Procedure:

  1. First, select the period "Selected period" of interest to derive streamflow statistics.
  2. Then, select the smoothing technique "N-day mean". When this is applied, the streamflow value of date t is the mean of streamflow from day t to t-n-1 (for example, to calculate n day lowflow smoothing technique can be used). Incase n = 1, no smoothing technique is applied.
  3. Then, select which "Streamflow statistic" should be used, maximum, mean, or minimum streamflow during the "Selected period".

Color scheme: The streamflow values calculated above will be compared with streamflow data from the same period of each year between 1980 and the most recent year available (2025, as of this app’s development). Based on this comparison, streamflow quantiles will be calculated. Depending on the focus—flood, drought, or general conditions—these quantiles will be translated into corresponding verbal descriptions, as outlined below (USGS WaterWatch):

  •  All
    • Highest: Highest value during the entire time
    • Above 90th percentile: Much above normal
    • 75th - 90th percentile: Above normal
    • 25th - 75th percentiles: Normal
    • 10th - 25th percentile: Below normal
    • Below 10th percentile: Much below normal
    • Lowest: Lowest value during the entire time
  • Flood
    • Above 99th percentile: Extreme hydrologic flood
    • 95th - 99th percentile: Servere hydrologic flood
    • Below 95th percentile: No flood
  • Drought
    • Above 25th percentile: No drought
    • 10th - 25th percentile: Below normal
    • 5th - 10th percentile: Moderate hydrologic drought
    • Below 5th percentile: Servere hydrologic drought
    • Lowest: Extreme hydrologic drought
